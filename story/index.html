Finding Home Again — transcript

Narrator: Home is more than a building. It is the people who welcome us, the memories we carry, and the feeling that we belong somewhere.

Maya: When the storm came, I thought I had lost everything. I could not imagine starting again. But my neighbours checked on me, shared what they had, and helped me take the first steps forward.

Narrator: Rebuilding did not happen in a day. Maya found support in her community and gradually created a new routine, one small decision at a time.

Maya: I learned that home is not only the place you leave behind. It can also be the place you build with others. Today, I feel hopeful about what comes next.

Narrator: This has been Finding Home Again, a StoryCast story about resilience, connection, and beginning again.
