OUR PURPOSE

Every voice has a story.

StoryCast brings people closer through audio and video storytelling. We believe meaningful stories should be easy to discover, experience, and understand.

Why StoryCast exists

Stories help us understand lives beyond our own. StoryCast is a storytelling microsite concept created to showcase personal experiences, different perspectives, and meaningful moments through audio and video.

Our goal is to make the experience welcoming for people with different abilities, devices, and ways of engaging with media.

DESIGNED FOR EVERYONE

Accessibility comes first

StoryCast aims to follow WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility principles. We use semantic HTML, keyboard-friendly navigation, visible focus indicators, readable text, and alternatives to audiovisual content.
